# BitesToSave

BitesToSave is a smart surplus food rescue platform designed to help connect surplus food from restaurants with nearby NGOs and shelters. The platform provides structured workflows for managing food donations, NGO listings, and pickup activities.

## Features

- Restaurant-side surplus food donation management
- NGO and shelter listings
- Food donation posting and management
- Pickup tracking and management
- Responsive dashboard interface
- Interactive user flows for food donations and pickups
- Structured donation and organization information
- Frontend and backend architecture
- User-friendly web interface

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Vite
- Node.js
- npm

Project Overview
BitesToSave focuses on reducing food wastage by providing a digital platform for managing surplus food donations and connecting them with organizations such as NGOs and shelters.
The platform is designed around three major workflows:
Food Donation — Restaurants can manage surplus food donation information.
NGO & Shelter Management — Organizations can be listed and managed through the platform.
Pickup Tracking — Donation pickup activities can be organized and tracked through structured user flows.

Objective
The primary objective of BitesToSave is to create a digital solution that can help reduce surplus food wastage while improving the coordination between food donors and organizations that can distribute the food to people in need.
